# 🎬 My Favorite Series & Movies

> *"So many shows, so little time — but these are the ones I keep coming back to."*
> — a very tired but happy college student

Hi! I'm a college student who watches way too many series in between deadlines (don't tell my professors 🤫). This README is basically my **watchlist hall of fame** — the shows and movies that live rent-free in my head.

---

## 📺 Favorite Series

### 1. **Black Clover** *(Anime)*
The ultimate underdog story. Asta has **zero magic** in a world where magic is everything, but he refuses to give up on becoming the Wizard King. It's loud, it's hype, and it hits different when you're stressed with school and need motivation. **Never give up** — that's the whole vibe. 🔥

### 2. **Doctor X** *(KDrama)*
A genius surgeon who's a total rebel — she doesn't play by the hospital's rules and *always* gets the job done. The medical cases are intense and the main character's confidence is honestly goals. Perfect for when you want something smart but still dramatic.

### 3. **Our Yesterday's Escape** *(University Series)*
A university series that feels way too real — friendships, school pressure, and all the "what am I even doing with my life" moments. Watching it felt like looking in a mirror as a student. 😅

### 4. **The Rain in España** *(University Series)*
Another university series that got me hooked! The romance and the *kilig* moments are top-tier, plus the campus setting makes it super relatable. Definitely a comfort watch.

---

## 🎥 Favorite Movie

### **Lilo & Stitch**
> *"Ohana means family. Family means nobody gets left behind or forgotten."*

A childhood classic I still rewatch. It's funny, heartfelt, and that quote above still gets me every single time. Stitch is chaotic and adorable — basically me during finals week.
